1 Executive Summary

In today’s competitive educational landscape, leading secondary schools require robust, modern digital infrastructure to manage admissions, streamline termly grade computation, reconcile tuition collections without manual leakages, and project institutional prestige to prospective parents.

This proposal presents the turnkey deployment of the New State High School Digital Campus System: a high-converting public presence, automated online admission engine, multi-role academic portal, Ministry-grade report card generator with QR verification, and financial ledger.

2 Scope of Deliverables & Implemented Modules

🌐 High-Converting Web Presence

10+ branded, fast-loading SEO pages, mobile sticky action bar (Call, WhatsApp, Apply), and NDPR child privacy policy.

📝 Online Admissions & Screening

Paperless prospective student registration, automated application reference generator, and CBT exam login portal.

📊 Academic Broadsheet & Grading

CA1 (20) + CA2 (20) + Exam (60) formula with instant WAEC/NECO automatic letter grade assignment (A1 to F9).

🖨️ A4 Official Report Card Generator

Lagos State Ministry-compliant continuous assessment report sheet with live QR verification and principal stamp seals.

💳 Bursar Fee Reconciliation

Student payment tracking, bank transfer slip verification, zero-leakage financial summaries, and ledger export.

🛡️ Enterprise Security Layer

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), 100% IDOR defense, NoSQL/ReDoS input sanitization, and anti-brute-force rate limiting.
